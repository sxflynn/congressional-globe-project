I regret that the bill which has passed botli Houses of Congress, entitled "An act to pro- tect all persons in the United States in their civil rights, and furnish the means for their vindication," contains provisions which I can- not approve, consistently with my sense of duty to the whole people and my obligations to the Constitution of the United States. I am there- fore constrained to return it to the Senate, the House in which it originated, with my objec- tions to its becoming a law.
