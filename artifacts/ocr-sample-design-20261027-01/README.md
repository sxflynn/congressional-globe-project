# T49: frozen sample and repair-evaluation design

The question is whether the repair method improves reading while preserving what the page actually says, including names and numbers, and how much verification it requires. This package freezes a small diagnostic sample and an independent image-reading plan. It creates no reference readings, accepted numerical standard, new review allowance or model-run permission. [Master task](../../MASTER_PLAN.md#ordered-task-queue).

## What is frozen

| Group | Allocation | Purpose and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |

It estimates outcomes for nonempty OCR paragraphs, with weights; it is not a representative word/page sample. |
| Reserved confirmation | 10whole paragraphs:2from each part | Later, separately authorized confirmation. Excludes primary source pages/text hashes and named historical exposure pages/text hashes. It describes a restricted frame; never pool its rates with the primary population estimate. |

[Design and preselection bounds](design.json) were written before source selection. [Sample identities](sample.json), [exposure record](exposure.json), [challenge specifications](challenges.json) and [scorecard](scorecard.json) are frozen together. The two probability groups have distinct source paragraphs and pages and no identical raw text across groups. Confirmation text is withheld from this package; its IDs, lengths and hashes are present. No reader or model should examine it for tuning before the method/criteria are frozen. Mechanical preparation processed source bytes; this is disclosed preparation exposure, not proof of never-seen content.

For each part, rank paragraphs by SHA256 of the UTF-8 string `seed + NUL + source_id`, breaking digest ties by source ID. `source_id` is `item_identifier:scan_leaf:paragraph_id`. Choose the lowest5priorities for primary using `globe-T49-20261027-primary-v1`. After freezing those IDs, construct each confirmation frame using the fixed exclusions, rank by the distinct seed `globe-T49-20261027-confirmation-v1` and choose2. No output-driven reranking, replacement or retry is permitted. This is reproducible pseudorandom-priority sampling; the stated inclusion probabilities use the uniform-priority model. Seeds were fixed before selection, not selected for favorable text.

Use N/5 to estimate primary totals, and ratios of weighted totals for error/opportunity rates. Do not average the five part rates equally and call that a corpus estimate. Words within a paragraph are clustered; uncertainty calculations must preserve that structure. Five paragraphs per part cannot establish rare-error safety or precise category-specific accuracy. Confirmation inclusion probability is2/M conditional on the primary selection and historical-exposure exclusions; its expansion weight is M/2 and applies only to that restricted remaining frame. Challenge/control cases receive no probability weights.

The primary sample contains2,857raw codepoints; all25targets fit the text cap. This is a deliberately small first diagnostic, not a statistically powered corpus-accuracy study. Uniform paragraph selection naturally includes short records. Do not replace them with longer debate because they look less useful. Report opportunities and uncertainty honestly; a larger sample would require a new frozen bounded design. Content-type coverage and rare-name/number coverage are not guaranteed by five-part allocation, which is why challenge results remain separately visible.

## Exposure and image limits

Named exposure inputs include earlier reading primary/confirmation/context units, the T47 inspected pages, the T43 raw/static pairs and frozen failure fixtures. Primary does not exclude previous exposure: otherwise its target would silently become a different population. None of the selected primary pages matches the named known exposure pages, but broader historical role/subject/search/operator exposure is incomplete. All records retain that unknown. Past model-training exposure is unknown. The reserved group excludes the recorded exposure pages and raw hashes as well as primary pages and raw hashes; no claim of completely unseen confirmation or held-out gold is made.

The manifest binds source IDs, full original/static text fingerprints, page-line fingerprints, page/paragraph geometry and saved edits for primary targets. Context is at most one complete preceding and following paragraph on the same page, capped separately; it is supplied evidence, excluded from target scoring. Chamber, turn and date continuity remain unverified. No cross-page discovery or metadata propagation is implied.

No new image was fetched or read. Primary/confirmation image hashes are unknown until actual cached/new bytes are bound to the selected targets and coverage/registration/legibility are checked. A cached crop somewhere on the same page is not proof of sufficient evidence. Proposed ceiling:2verified crops per unit,86crop bindings and64MiB total for the43units, including reused bindings; actual fetches/costs must be separately frozen and authorized. Missing, irrelevant, unreadable or insufficient images remain unresolved cases. Do not substitute easier paragraphs or accept model agreement as evidence.

## Whole-unit reference plan

If separately authorized, obtain two image-first readings of every evaluated whole target. Readers receive the registered image and frozen context, with no OCR, static repair, detector flag, suggestion or model output shown until their readings are recorded and frozen separately. Record reader identity/type, prior exposure, image hashes, source-unit identity, exact transcription, uncertain/unreadable regions, completeness and actual elapsed time. Image-first means the reader starts from print; it reduces suggestion bias but cannot erase earlier exposure. Readers must record when wording/layout is ambiguous rather than invent it.

Then align readings to OCR and derived text using source mappings. Preserve both original readings and every disagreement; adjudicate from the image within the separate time cap. Unresolved disagreements remain unresolved. Completeness includes every word/number in the target, not only proposed edits. Context is not extra scored text. A damaged OCR paragraph may omit print within its registered image region: record that discrepancy explicitly; text missing outside the defined image/target scope is not covered by paragraph recall. Reference alignment/tokenization must be frozen before scoring, including punctuation, split words and legitimate spelling variants.

Two AI readers provide provisional AI evidence, not two human judgments or historical gold; using the repairer/auditor as its own reference is prohibited. If human readings are desired, their new effort requires a concrete separate decision. If only one reading is available, preserve it with that limitation and withhold resolved efficacy/recall claims under the proposed two-reading rule. Synthetic constructed references test software/control behavior only; they do not count as independently read historical print.

## Exact ceilings and the effort tradeoff

Each unit permits at most12,000raw target codepoints and12,000static target codepoints, plus4,000context codepoints in each layer. Across43units the maximum is688,000codepoints per layer /1,376,000combined. Context is complete or omitted, never silently clipped. Oversize selected targets keep their full identity/hash/length and `over_budget_unresolved`; do not truncate and then call the remaining text a complete unit. The sample manifest has a16MiB ceiling and currently contains about128KiB. Historical challenge targets also fit their per-unit cap. Synthetic controls have no historical image evidence.

Proposed reading allowance is at most5minutes per reader per unit, plus2minutes adjudication per unit. For two readers, primary25costs at most250reader-minutes plus50adjudication minutes; confirmation10adds100+20; all8challenge/control units allow80+16. That is a conservative maximum of516minutes across both readers and adjudication, not516minutes requested from Stephen. Synthetic controls need no human transcription; they remain inside this ceiling rather than consuming an automatic allowance. Stop at the caps and retain incomplete coverage instead of extending time. Actual reader availability, reading speed, tools and costs are unknown. This makes the cost of independent checking visible before adoption; lower effort changes evidence strength and must be explicitly recorded.

**No part of that proposed effort is authorized by T49.** HR-009/HR-010 remains at its existing10minutes/max3fixed-output round. This package does not register a packet, ask for additional readings or consume the allowance. H1/H2 govern standards/calibration and a separate concrete reference-reading decision. Live model/image spending still needs T55/T56 readiness/authority. This design does not choose a model budget or reopen answered questions.

## Scorecard and proposed decision rules

The unscored scorecard keeps detector misses/false flags, correct/harmful repairs, auditor misses/false alarms, uncertainty and names/places/years/other numbers separate. Count errors from independent whole-target references, not from the detector's suggestions. Known-word, plausible-number and apparently successful static units receive the same whole-unit inspection. If no selected unit ends up detector-negative, report that fact; do not manufacture population controls by substituting units. Synthetic/exposed controls test negative behavior separately. A model's no-finding response reports only its inspected scope; it is not an error-free certificate.

For repair benefit, use all independently established original errors as denominator and retain unchanged/missed/abstained cases. For harm, report newly incorrect readings among previously correct opportunities and among attempted edits. For auditor recall, use independently established residual discrepancies, including unflagged/apparent-success failures. Keep each source defect once for the main span count; overlapping category signals may be listed separately without inflating that count. Exact opportunity/token conventions and ratio-uncertainty method must be versioned before scoring under T52/T55.

Require two complete readings before a unit contributes to resolved efficacy ratios under the proposed rule. Still report every selected missing/partial/over-budget unit and its weighted mass. Label resolved-subset rates as conditional on evaluability. For unit-level error incidence, show lower/upper missing-outcome bounds rather than imputing missing units as clean; word/error-count bounds are unavailable unless opportunities are finite and defined. Unknown source readings never silently become correct, wrong or omitted.

Propose holding the method recommendation on any independently confirmed harmful change to a name, place, number/value, meaning or citation; zero observed consequential harm is a proposed gate, not an agreed standard or proof of safety. Also propose no tolerated consequential misses in fully inspected challenge cases, while reporting every miss and uncertainty. Population recall, acceptable unresolved rate, provider/runtime costs and broader thresholds remain unagreed. H1/H2 must agree standards before acceptance scoring. No numerical success target is invented here as human policy.

Matched raw/static/targeted-repair/audit outputs must retain exact source support. Search, metadata, grounded answers and reading/TTS remain separate outcomes; this package defines what to report, not new query labels or measured task quality. No audio is produced/listened to, so TTS is unmeasured. CPU, model seconds/calls/tokens, image bytes, failures, reader/adjudication time and unknown hardware/energy costs stay separate. Optional prediction remains off the cleanup critical path.
