# T48: vocabulary and detector options

A spell checker can flag unfamiliar wording; it cannot establish what the page prints. Recommend the preserved local vocabulary plus explicit word/name/number signals for the first offline sample. Keep the existing static repair arm fixed and implement any new detector as a separate, versioned library under T52. No dependency or external dataset is adopted by this research. [Master task](../../MASTER_PLAN.md#ordered-task-queue).

The documented input recipe reproduces the saved 3,507,065-byte vocabulary exactly in memory: SHA256 `fef27d9e883ae3f84f440918ca7b37a627b2f1327445e5418b80006fce44a11d`. [Local identities and check](local-inputs.json) record all inputs. `/usr/share/dict/words` resolves to `web2`; it has 235,976 current lines, `web2a` 76,205, `propernames` 1,308 and the project supplement 44. The saved union has 312,226 lines, including its blank line. Case folding produces 310,676 distinct forms, as recorded by T47. Filter blank entries for detector lookup without rewriting the saved resource.

The local README identifies Webster's Second International as the base and describes modern American spelling maintenance, including removal of archaic forms. Its supplier's copyright-expiration statement does not establish a portable redistribution license for this assembled file. No independent license clearance is claimed. Use the already present bytes for local comparison; packaging them remains unresolved. The list is neither an 1865–1866 vocabulary standard nor a complete congressional-name/place list. The suffix heuristic in `static_cleanup.py` and its ASCII line-wrap patterns do not supply general morphology, proper-name recognition or contextual number verification.

## Proposed signal and routing policy

This is a design recommendation for T49/T52, not a selected model-routing rule or approved numerical threshold. Preserve exact raw Unicode text and positions; all normalization below produces lookup keys only. Keep versioned rule/resource hashes, category, signals, reason and explicit unknowns for every retained candidate. Resource unavailable means `not_assessed`, not dictionary miss. Keep original case, punctuation and spacing visible in evidence.

| Category | Proposed signal behavior | Routing and controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary words | Case-folded exact membership plus separate punctuation/split/low-confidence signals. Unknown word means unfamiliar to this resource. Preserve apostrophe/diacritic/hyphen distinctions in raw support; document lookup variants. A known word may still be wrong in context. | New detector flags review candidates; it never rewrites from a dictionary suggestion. Include known-word errors and known-correct rare/historical forms. |
| Names and places | Separate name/place cues from ordinary dictionary membership, including titles, initials, capitalization/context and any available auxiliary matches. All-caps text alone is not a person detector. Unsupported lookup or identity stays unknown. | Retain name/place verification candidates even if dictionary-known. Do not auto-correct to the closest roster name. Correct rare names and clear unmatched names are essential controls. |
| Years, amounts and other numbers | Recognize digit groups, dates, money, vote counts, bill/chapter references and Roman numerals with context. Syntax/plausibility does not verify a value. Ambiguous word/Roman forms may carry multiple categories. | Every recognized numeric span carries `value_unverified` until independently checked; this does not automatically send every span to AI. T49/T55 must compare the cost and misses of chosen review routes. Include plausible-but-wrong values and correct unusual values. |
| Mixed alphanumeric / symbol damage | Flag unexpected within-word mixtures, broken glyph patterns and uncertainty about tokenization; do not treat conventional abbreviations, identifiers or legitimate numeral notation as errors by rule. | Preserve raw anchor/context; no global character substitution. Compare confirmed damage with correct identifier/abbreviation controls. |
| Line wraps and hyphens | Existing adjacent-line/vocabulary join is the preserved static comparator. New detector can record unresolved split candidates and edits already applied, with separate raw and derived positions. | Inspect apparent successful joins as well as unresolved ones. Include meaningful hyphens, repeated words and wrong-but-vocabulary-known joins. Reject unsupported cross-paragraph relocation. |

Use deterministic membership/pattern signals as proposed library inputs rather than a new aggregate “clean” score. T52 must freeze exact tokenizer/rule configuration and negative behavior in tests; no runtime or detection accuracy follows from this recommendation. Proposed detector signals must not silently change the saved static arm, let dictionary presence waive image checking, or alter accepted text.

## Unflagged controls and evaluation

T49 should freeze representative source units independently of detector output, plus separately reported challenge/control cases. Inspect whole selected units, including text with no lexical flags, before estimating missed errors. The same independent image-reference scope must cover apparent successful edits, unedited names/numbers, real-word substitutions and known-correct unfamiliar forms. Missing/unreadable images stay unresolved, with no silent replacement or success grade. Detector-negative means no rule fired, never verified correct.

Retain category-specific counts for errors found/missed, correct text flagged, correct repairs, newly harmful readings, abstentions and unresolved evidence. Separate names/places/years/other numbers and multiple overlapping signals; avoid counting one damaged span as several independent errors. Report original counts/denominators and sample weights where applicable. Whole-unit references are necessary for recall; absent references mean recall unavailable. Sample all-zero/low-signal units as controls without letting a page average hide one consequential numeric error. Optional predictive scoring remains off the cleanup critical path.

No extra human review or provider calls follow from this policy. T49 freezes bounds and proposes reference effort; T50 defines repair/audit contracts. The study still needs source-image readings independent of the repairer/auditor, agreed criteria and finite launch authority. A historical dictionary gap should be reported and tested through unknown/rare-word controls, not filled by invented vocabulary or model guesses.
